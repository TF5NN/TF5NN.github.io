## What it is for

The aim, as written in the project's decision log: a simple, easy-to-understand planner that helps new hams get into satellite work with a VGC VR-N76 and a 2 m / 70 cm Yagi, without a huge list of satellites that are broken or out of reach.

The reasons behind the defaults are recorded in the [decision log on GitHub](https://github.com/TF5NN/sat-track/blob/main/docs/DECISIONS.md).
