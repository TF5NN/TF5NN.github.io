A small amateur-satellite planner designed for the VGC VR-N76 and a 2 m / 70 cm Yagi. The pass, frequency and Doppler guidance also applies to other FM half-duplex dual-band radios.

## What it can do

- **Passes for your location**, worked out on your own device from a Maidenhead locator or coordinates.
- **Opens on what you can work**: FM voice and APRS. Receive-only services are one tap away.
- **Radio settings for each pass**: frequencies, CTCSS tone and Doppler steps in 5 kHz jumps.
- **Sky path and bearings**, with times in local time and UTC.
- **Reception reports** from AMSAT for each service, with a warning when nobody has heard a satellite lately.
- **Export** a pass to your calendar with the radio settings, or the current orbit lines for the radio's app.

## Good to know

A pass in the list means the satellite is above your horizon. It does not prove the satellite is transmitting. Bearings assume a clear horizon.

Your location and the pass calculations stay in your browser.
