1. [Open the app](https://tf5nn.github.io/sat-track/). It asks for your location as a Maidenhead locator (4, 6 or 8 characters) or as coordinates. The last station you used is remembered in that browser.
2. The list shows the passes you can work with an FM handheld: FM voice and APRS, with a peak of at least 5° above the horizon.
3. Choose **All** or **Listen** to include services you can only receive.
4. Pick a pass to see its times, bearings, sky path, frequencies, CTCSS tone and Doppler steps.
5. Save the pass to your calendar, or download the orbit lines to enter in the radio's app.

## Reading the times

Times are shown in your device's local time with UTC next to them. You can switch to UTC only, and the choice is remembered.

## Doppler

The Doppler settings are listed as 5 kHz radio steps, only as many as the pass needs. Retune when the drift passes 3.5 kHz.
