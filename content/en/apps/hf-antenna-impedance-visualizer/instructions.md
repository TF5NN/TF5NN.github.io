1. [Open the app](https://tf5nn.github.io/HF-Antenna-Impedance-Visualizer/). It runs in any modern browser, with nothing to install.
2. Choose **End Fed** or **Dipole** with the buttons at the top of the control panel.
3. Switch on the bands and matching zones you care about.
4. Move the pointer over the graph to read the impedance and SWR for every active band at that point.

## End-Fed mode

- Turn on **★ Sweet Spots** to find wire lengths that work on several bands.
- Drag the inspect pin, or type a length, to lock a measurement at an exact wire length.
- Turn on the counterpoise to see how its length moves the match. The faint curves show the wire alone for comparison.

## Dipole mode

- Use the feedpoint slider or the preset buttons (50%, 33%, 25%) to compare centre-fed and off-centre-fed antennas.
- Click on the graph to set the feedpoint position directly.
- The coloured strip at the bottom shows how much common-mode trouble to expect: green near the centre, red at extreme off-centre positions.
