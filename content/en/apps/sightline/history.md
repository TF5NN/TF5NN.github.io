<p class="note">This page is waiting for the story: why you built it, what went wrong along the way, and what you learned. The dates below come from the project's history on GitHub.</p>

## Milestones

| Date | What changed |
|------|--------------|
| 16 Nov 2026 | First version: the terrain profile between two points, read from ArcticDEM |
| 16 Nov 2026 | Line of sight, Fresnel zone, radio link estimate and "smart" 2 m detail |
| 16 Nov 2026 | First checks against real links: one 143 km path on 2 m, and 91 MeshCore repeater pairs on 868 MHz |
| 17 Nov 2026 | Station layers, starting with ÍRA's repeaters, and stations coloured by what point A can work |
| 17 Nov 2026 | Look around: the skyline from point A |
| 18 Nov 2026 | Dark mode, and the TF5NN style guide's colours |
| 18 Nov 2026 | In Iceland, the first draft of a profile comes from the shared terrain files, for about a tenth of the download |

How far to trust the model, and what it has been checked against, is on the app's own [How it works](https://tf5nn.github.io/sightline/how.html) page. The full write-up is in [docs/validation.md on GitHub](https://github.com/TF5NN/sightline/blob/main/docs/validation.md).
