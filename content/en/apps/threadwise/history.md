<p class="note">This page is waiting for the story: why you made the spreadsheet, what it was used for, and what you learned.</p>

## Milestones

| Date | What changed |
|------|--------------|
| 5 Oct 2021 | First version: an Excel sheet for measuring metric threads over wires, with manual deviations |
| 19 Nov 2026 | The spreadsheet learns the ISO 965-1 tables, with the standard's formulas for the gaps |
| 20 Nov 2026 | Threadwise: the spreadsheet becomes a web app with Measure, Make and Find, in English and Icelandic, with every number checked against published tables |
| 20 Nov 2026 | The *Töflubók* becomes a third source and the Icelandic follows it; Tr, S (buttress) and DIN 158 metric taper threads join, 322 threads in eleven families |
| 20 Nov 2026 | Workshop order: Find → Make → Measure, with one tap to carry a thread from tab to tab |
