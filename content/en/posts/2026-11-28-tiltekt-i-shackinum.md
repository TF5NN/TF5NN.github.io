## The shelves

Today's project was tidying up the shack.

![The desk before tidying up, with the station and cables everywhere](/media/2025-12-shack-1.jpg)

I'd been looking for a long time at what shelves might suit this project and ended up with a height extension unit for Billy bookcases. Its advantages are that the extension has no bottom, the back is a proper panel (not cardboard), it's designed so you can mount another extension on top if you need more shelves, and the dimensions work well. The shelf is 36×26 cm inside, which might just about be deep enough for a full-size rig (IC-7300).

![The sides of the extension unit with new holes for the shelves](/media/2025-12-shack-2.jpg)

I had to add holes for shelves because it was only designed for shelves in the middle, and then I had to drill the whole thing full of holes to get the cables and leads where they needed to go.

![The extension unit with large cable holes and a spade bit in the drill](/media/2025-12-shack-3.jpg)

![The rig, power supply, antenna tuner and amplifier on the shelves](/media/2025-12-shack-4.jpg)

Now I'm waiting for a 10" screen from Uncle Ali (AliExpress) that goes on top of the shelf and will show HamClock.

## Brackets for the HamClock screen
