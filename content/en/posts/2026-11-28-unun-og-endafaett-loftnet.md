Building a 49:1 UnUn and an 80–10 m end-fed half-wave antenna, all with parts from Sotabeams.

The antenna design is by VK3IL: [Multiband end-fed 80–10 m antenna](https://vk3il.net/projects-antenna/multiband-end-fed-80-10m-antenna/). I chose the core after reading up on MM0PX's experiments (see the links near the bottom of the [Sotabeams page](https://www.sotabeams.co.uk/toroid-2643251002-type-43/)). The box is a design by AH6K on [Thingiverse](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:6762745), which I modified slightly: I drilled out the hole for the BNC connector.
