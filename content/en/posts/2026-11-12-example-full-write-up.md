This is an example of a long post. It is here to show what headings, photos, lists and links look like. Delete it when you have a real post of your own.

## Headings split a long post into parts

A line starting with `##` becomes a heading like the one above. Normal paragraphs are just text with an empty line between them. You can make words **bold** or *italic*, and add [a link](https://github.com/TF5NN) to anything.

## Photos

A photo can have a caption. The site shrinks large photos by itself, so a picture straight from a phone still loads quickly.

![Placeholder picture standing in for a workbench photo](/media/example-bench.jpg "A caption goes here. Leave it out if the photo needs no explanation.")

## Lists and settings

Lists are useful for parts and steps:

- Filament: PETG
- Layer height: 0.2 mm
- Print time: about 3 hours

1. Print the two halves.
2. Press in the threaded inserts.
3. Bolt it together.

Settings or commands can go in a box so they are easy to copy:

```
M104 S240 ; set the nozzle temperature
```
