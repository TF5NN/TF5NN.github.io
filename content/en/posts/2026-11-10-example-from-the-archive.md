This example shows a post written long after the fact. It was posted in November 2026, but "worked on" is set to *Summer 2022*.

The site works out the gap by itself and adds the note "Written up 4 years later", so nobody is misled about when it happened. On the [timeline](/timeline/) this post sits under 2022.

The "worked on" line is free text. All of these work:

- `2022`
- `Summer 2022`
- `March–June 2022`
- `2019–2021`
- `14 August 2022`

This post is also the example of a translation. This English file is the original, and a file with the same name sits in the Icelandic folder. That is why the language switch at the top of the page flips between the two.
