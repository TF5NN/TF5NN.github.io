My name is Gunnar B. Guðlaugsson. I’m a machinist, a mechanical engineering technologist, and a licensed radio amateur (TF5NN) since 2024.

This site is meant as a kind of diary where I keep track of what I’m tinkering with in my hobbies. The bar for what I post is low: sometimes just a photo or two with a short explanation, sometimes longer write-ups, whether about something recent or a project from long ago. 

It is also the front door to the [apps](/apps/) I have made.

## How to read the dates

Every post has two dates.

- **Posted** is the day the post went on this site.
- **Worked on** is when the thing actually happened. For older projects this can be vague, such as "Spring 2022" or "2019–2021", because I do not always remember the exact days.

When a post is about something from an earlier year, it carries a small note saying how much later it was written up. The [timeline](/timeline/) lists everything by the year the work happened.

## Two languages

I write in Icelandic or English, whichever suits the post. A post that exists in only one language still appears on both versions of the site, with a label saying so. Translated posts say which language they were translated from.
